1. Defense lawyer perspective: what weaknesses will they exploit?
2. Judge perspective: how might the court "和稀泥" (split the difference / protect status quo)?
3. Developer perspective: how might the parent company operate behind the scenes?

**Why:** Olina said "确保我的准备是充分的，不会陷入信息和角度的自high" — she wants to stress-test her position, not hear cheerleading.

**How to apply:** Every strategy document should include a "reality check" section. Flag weaknesses honestly. When something "认为物业违法" is an assertion rather than established fact, say so clearly. Present probability estimates for outcomes.
